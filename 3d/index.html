Measuring what people judge by feel.

Three projects. Each one takes something people argue about on instinct (a film, a fare, a shot under pressure) and puts a number on it.

01 · RPDI

How far a film drifts from the record.

16 history films, scored claim by claim. Each frame stands further back the more its film distorts. RRR is furthest, at 0.796. All the President's Men is closest, at 0.096.

02 · ADMI

What a flight costs when nobody else flies it.

Every nonstop route out of Atlanta. The higher the arc, the further the fare sits above a fair price for the distance. The 18 red routes stay expensive on every date. 17 of them are Delta only.

03 · DTM

How good a decision is under pressure.

Every ball of a limited-overs innings carries a pressure weight: 1 in the first two overs, 5 in the last two. DTM scores the decision, not whether it came off.
